Revise the existing Get Plugged website design. Keep the current bold editorial typography, strong hierarchy, grid-based layout, and culture-forward energy, but reposition the brand away from looking like a concert promoter, nightlife company, or production company.

Get Plugged supports emerging artists, creators, and local brands through connections, resources, creative guidance, collaborations, and experiences. Events are part of the brand, but they are not the entire identity.

IMPORTANT: The purple backgrounds currently shown in the design are temporary placeholders only. Replace ALL purple backgrounds and purple-tinted sections throughout the entire website. Purple is not part of the Get Plugged brand palette.

VISUAL DIRECTION

* Replace all purple and black-dominant backgrounds with a soft cream background
* Main background: approximately #F1ECE3
* Main text: dark charcoal, approximately #262321
* Keep the official Get Plugged green #40B648 as an accent color
* Use green selectively for buttons, small labels, arrows, underlines, hover states, and occasional highlighted words
* Do not use large bright-green backgrounds
* Avoid neon, futuristic, cyber, or Y2K styling
* Avoid styling that resembles the 20Titanz website
* Keep the site young, editorial, culture-forward, warm, current, and elevated
* Use photography to bring in additional color naturally

The overall color balance should feel primarily like:
cream background + dark typography + restrained Get Plugged green

OVERALL GOAL

The finished website should present Get Plugged as a young, connected resource and network for emerging artists, creators, and local brands.

It should feel like a place people come when they already have momentum but need access to the right people, resources, guidance, experiences, or opportunities for their next step.

Keep the existing design’s boldness and personality. Change the positioning, copy, imagery, and color balance rather than turning it into a generic corporate website.
